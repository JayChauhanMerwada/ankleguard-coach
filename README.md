A responsive, mobile-first, dark-mode single-page web application designed for a 27-year-old male (182 cm / 6'0", starting at 93 kg with a target of 75 kg and a 3-month milestone of 84 kg) with a **Left Ankle Post-ORIF (March 2026)** status (medial plate, fibular rod, 2 intact syndesmotic screws).

## 🛡️ Core Orthopedic Architecture & Safeguards
- **Daily Pre-Workout Pain Gate (0–10 Slider):**
  - Scores 0–3/10: Cleared for daily lifting with strict 8–10 min Ankle Prep.
  - Scores ≥ 4/10: **Automatic Safety Override.** Instantly swaps all lower-body exercises and treadmill walking for seated chest-supported upper-body/core drills and recumbent stationary cycling.
- **Mandatory 8–10 Min Ankle Prep Block:**
  1. Seated Ankle Pumps & Controlled Circles (2x15 + 10 circles/dir)
  2. Resistance-Band Ankle Eversion with 2s hold (3x15) - Activates peroneus longus & brevis to eliminate outer foot rolling.
  3. Gentle Seated Towel Calf/Soleus Stretch (2x25s, stop before anterior pinch).
  4. Supported Big-Toe-Grounded Heel Raises (2x12, 80% pressure through 1st Metatarsal head).
- **Strictly Forbidden List:** Zero running/jogging, jump rope, box jumps, burpees, heavy barbell squats, lunges, standing calf raises, unstable pads/BOSU, or treadmill inclines > 0%.

## 🏋️ 6-Day Weekly Split & 3-Month Phase Engine
- **Month 1 (Weeks 1–4):** Neuromuscular adaptation, 10–12 reps, 3-1-1-0 tempo, joint conditioning.
- **Month 2 (Weeks 5–8):** Dense strength, 5–8 reps, progressive overload (+10–15%), 75–90s rest.
- **Month 3 (Weeks 9–12):** Antagonist power supersets, crisp concentric drive, tight waist definition.

### Days:
- **Monday:** Upper Push (Incline DB Press, Machine Press, Seated Shoulder Press, Lateral Raise, Triceps Pushdown) + Core (Dead Bugs, Pallof Press) + Flat 0% Treadmill Walk.
- **Tuesday:** Lower A Quad & Glute (Vertical-Shin Hip Thrust, Seated Leg Extension, High-Foot Leg Press, Seated Hip Abduction) + Core + Stationary Bike.
- **Wednesday:** Upper Pull & Desk Posture Reset (Neutral-Grip Lat Pulldown, Chest-Supported Row, Single-Arm Cable Row, Face Pulls, Incline Hammer Curls) + Flat Walk.
- **Thursday:** Lower B Hamstring & Pelvic Stability (Hamstring Curl, 45° Back Extension, Seated Adduction, Single-Leg Bridge) + Core + Stationary Bike.
- **Friday:** Upper Athletic Conditioning Supersets (Flat DB Press + Wide Pulldown, Machine Shoulder Press + Cable Row, Lateral Raise + Overhead Triceps) + Core + Flat Walk.
- **Saturday:** Posture Reset, Deep Core & Ankle Armor (Peroneal Band Holds, Cable Y-Raises, Seated Pallof Iso-Hold, Seated March Drill) + 25 min Zone-2 Conditioning.
- **Sunday:** Full Recovery Checklist (swelling audit, hydration, sleep, weekly weigh-in & waist log).
